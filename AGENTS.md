# NorthStar Codex Instructions

## Governing context

Before architecture or implementation work, read these files in order:

1. `docs/architecture/NorthStar_Architecture_Decision_Register.md`
2. `docs/project/NorthStar_Project_Seed.md`
3. Relevant records under `docs/architecture/decisions/`

Treat entries marked **Confirmed** in the decision register as settled unless a concrete conflict exists with a higher-authority source listed in the register. Preserve **Deferred** items unless their recorded trigger has occurred. Proposed ADRs are not yet Accepted.

Ask only unresolved, implementation-blocking questions, one at a time. Record each newly approved architecture decision in the decision register and, where appropriate, in an ADR. Do not silently promote Provisional, Deferred, Open, or Proposed items.

Do not scaffold a major framework choice until its architecture decision permits it. Preserve unrelated user changes. After implementation, run the relevant checks and report what was verified.
